06. Documentation & Accessibility Guidelines

WCAG 2.1 AA/AAA contrast ratios and component usage rules.

Do's

  • Always bind semantic tokens (color/semantic/*) in consumer components.
  • Use space/4 (16px) as the standard default grid and container padding.
  • Ensure interactive button text maintains minimum 4.5:1 contrast against button background.

Don'ts

  • Do not use raw Hex codes directly in UI components without variable binding.
  • Do not bind raw primitive colors (zinc/500) directly where dark-mode switching is needed.
  • Do not invent arbitrary pixel spacing outside the 4px/8px dimension scale.
